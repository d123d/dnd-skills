# Tabletop & Fantasy Roleplay

Guide for vivid, grounded, believable character portrayal—whether voicing one PC/companion or juggling a room full of distinct NPCs.

## Anti-Collapse Rules (Why AI Roleplay Fails)

1. **Never sound like one default narrator in five hats.** Every recurring character needs an identifiable speech signature.
2. **Never state internal emotion directly as dialogue** (*"I am deeply conflicted and distrust you"*). Convey distrust through guarded answers, subject changes, posture, and demands.
3. **Never give characters omniscient helpfulness.** NPCs act from incomplete rumors, self-interest, prejudice, debt, and fear—not a desire to dump quest exposition.
4. **Never seize player agency.** React to what the player character just did; never script the player's feelings, reply, or decisions.

## 60-Second Character Voice Card

Define before speaking for any named NPC or PC:

- **Want Right Now:** Immediate goal in this scene (avoid death, keep a secret, skim extra gold, test loyalty).
- **Obstacle / Leverage:** What stops them, and what actually moves them (bribe, threat, oath, vanity, guilt).
- **Speech Signature (pick 2):** Sentence length (clipped vs winding), vocabulary register (sailor slang, legalistic, courtly, rustic), verbal tic or pet phrase, topic they refuse to touch.
- **Physical Anchor:** One concrete action they do while talking (wiping a mug, shuffling dice, scanning exits, nursing stiff knuckles).
- **Hidden Truth (DM-only):** One fact they omit unless pressured or persuaded.

## Portraying Multi-NPC Scenes

- Give two speakers **opposing priorities or worldviews** so they react to each other, not only to the PC.
- Differentiate by **syntax first, accents rarely**: phonetic dialects slow reading down; word choice and rhythm stay crystal clear.
- Mark speaker shifts visually via action beats before or after lines instead of stacked *"X said / Y replied"* tags.

## Attitude & Trust Progression

Track NPC stance across turns (`Hostile -> Wary -> Neutral -> Warm -> Loyal`) and shift it ONLY for concrete causes:

- **Hard shift down:** Broken promise, public humiliation, caught theft, unprovoked threat against an ally.
- **Earned shift up:** Risking skin for them, honoring their creed/custom, paying fair or delivering sensitive proof.
- **Roleplay check alignment:** Let persuasion/deception/intimidation checks modify *outcome degrees*, while letting strong player arguments adjust DC or grant Advantage.

## Turn Output Discipline (During Active Play)

- Deliver spoken lines + observable body language + sensory cues cleanly (typically 2–6 sentences per beat unless a formal speech fits).
- Stop cleanly when the player has a clear conversational opening or pressure point to answer.
- Never summarize *"meanwhile the whole tavern whispers your legend"* after every mundane conversation.
